homeThing C6 Factory Test

Test an assembled homeThing C6 board with the factory firmware. You need no computer and no Wi-Fi credentials.

Download homething-c6-provisioning.factory.bin, which is always the newest firmware release. Erase the flash first, so that the board has no saved Wi-Fi network. Then write the file at offset 0x0 over USB:

esptool.py --chip esp32c6 erase_flash
esptool.py --chip esp32c6 write_flash 0x0 homething-c6-provisioning.factory.bin

This firmware also does the Wi-Fi setup for the customer. Do not give the board a network during the test, because the board then installs the full firmware and the self-test is gone.

Equipment

You need:

  • A USB-C cable and a 5 V USB power supply.
  • A charged 1-cell Li-ion battery with a Molex PicoBlade 51021-0200 plug for J1.
  • An IR remote control, for example a TV remote.
  • A phone with a camera.

Some phone main cameras block infrared light. The front camera of most phones shows it.

Step 1Power on and boot animation

If the ring stays dark at the battery, connect USB power instead. If the board then starts, the battery is flat or J1 has a fault.

After the boot animation the ring rests at a dim white. That resting glow shows that the board is awake and that every LED still lights.

  1. Connect the charged battery to J1. The board starts.
  2. Watch the boot animation.
  3. Connect USB power. The board keeps USB power until step 6.
  • Pass: The wave goes clockwise two times, and each LED lights with its own color.
  • Fail: An LED stays dark in both laps. That LED or its connection in the chain has a fault.

Step 2Buttons and wheel

Each wheel step moves one white LED one position round the ring, in the direction of the turn.

Each press puts one color on the top half of the ring and another on the bottom half. The pair identifies the input, and the section "Color reference" gives every pair.

  1. Press each button, SW1 to SW11, for less than 1 s.
  2. Press each wheel direction for less than 1 s: up, down, left, right, and the wheel center.
  3. Turn the wheel one step clockwise.
  4. Turn the wheel one step counterclockwise.
  • Pass: Every input changes the ring, and each one gives its own pair of colors.
  • Fail: An input does not change the ring. That switch or its expander line has a fault.

Step 3Microphone

Do not cover the microphone hole, which the diagram marks.

In the VU meter, a green bar grows clockwise from D2 with the sound level. The last LED of the ring is red, not green. Unlit positions show dim blue.

  1. Press and hold the wheel center for 2 s. The ring changes to the VU meter.
  2. Speak toward the front of the board, at a distance of approximately 30 cm.
  3. Press and hold the wheel center for 2 s again. The ring goes back to its resting glow.
  • Pass: The bar moves with your voice.
  • Fail: The bar does not move. The microphone or its sound hole has a fault.

Step 4IR receive

  1. Hold the board with its back toward the IR remote, at a distance of 0.3 m to 1 m.
  2. Press a button on the IR remote.
  • Pass: The ring flashes white one time for each press of the remote.
  • Fail: The ring does not flash after three presses.

Step 5IR transmit

Dim magenta at D6 shows only that the firmware sent the bursts. An external receiver must confirm the light from D1.

Use a second board when the station has one, because a camera answer depends on the phone. The second board receives the bursts as an IR frame, which also passes step 4 on that board.

The ring stays dark during the transmit, so a phone camera can set its exposure for D1.

Second board

Put a second board that passed step 1 in front of the first board, with its back toward D1 at a distance of 0.3 m to 1 m.

  1. Press and hold SW1 for 2 s. Release it when D6 turns dim magenta.
  2. Watch the second board for 3 s.

Phone

Start the camera and aim it at D1 on the top edge of the board.

  1. Press and hold SW1 for 2 s. Release it when D6 turns dim magenta.
  2. Look at the phone screen for 3 s.
  • Pass: The ring of the second board flashes white.
  • Fail: The second board does not answer. Move it closer and try one more time.
  • Pass: On the phone screen, D1 flashes 15 times.
  • Fail: The phone screen shows no light from D1.

Step 6Battery

Step 1 connected USB power. Disconnect it here, because the battery check accepts only a reading on battery power.

The disconnection also gives the orange flash. Step 7 connects USB power again for the cyan flash.

  1. Disconnect USB power. Keep the battery connected. The ring flashes orange one time.
  2. Wait 10 s.
  • Pass: The board stays on, and the ring stays lit.
  • Fail: The board turns off when you disconnect USB power. This shows a fault at the battery or at J1.
  • Fail: The ring goes dark, or it flashes and the board restarts.

Step 7USB power

  1. Connect USB power again. Keep the battery connected.
  • Pass: The ring flashed orange at step 6, then cyan at this step.
  • Fail: A connection or a disconnection gives no flash.

Step 8Sleep and wake

A wake fills the ring one LED at a time, in the boot animation colors, then holds the complete ring.

A cold boot moves one pulse round the ring instead. That pulse at this step means that the board reset.

Every button and wheel direction shares one wake line, so any of them wakes the board.

This sleep can start with USB power connected.

  1. Keep the battery and USB power connected.
  2. Hold SW3 for 3 s, until the ring flashes dark blue.
  3. Release all buttons and wheel directions after the dark blue flash.
  4. After the ring goes dark, wait 5 s for the shutdown to end.
  5. Press and hold any button until the boot animation starts. Then release it.
  6. Wait for the boot animation to end.
  • Pass: The ring went dark, and the button press filled the ring one LED at a time.
  • Fail: The ring flashes red. The board refused the sleep, so release SW3 and hold it again.
  • Fail: The board does not sleep, or it does not wake on a button press.
  • Fail: One pulse travels round the ring. The board reset instead of waking.

Result

The board passes only when every step passes. Write the result of each step as you go.

If a step fails, continue with the next step. A board that fails one step still gives useful information about the others.

Parts not covered

This procedure finds no fault in these parts:

  • The Qwiic connector J2, because that check needs a known Qwiic device at the station.
  • The battery charge circuit, because that check takes too long for the station.
  • The Wi-Fi radio and the Bluetooth radio. The antenna is part of the U1 module, which the module supplier tests before assembly.

Shipped state

To ship a board after the test, disconnect USB power. If the battery stays connected, the board sleeps after 5 minutes.

The test does not change the flash, but Wi-Fi setup stores the network there. If a board received a Wi-Fi network or the full firmware, do these steps:

  1. Erase the full flash with esptool.py --chip esp32c6 erase_flash.
  2. Write the factory firmware at offset 0x0, as the start of this procedure shows.

Color reference

These tables hold the colors that the steps use.

Boot animation colors

Step 1 shows this color at each LED, in chain order. A cold boot moves one pulse round the ring. A wake fills the ring with the same colors.

LED Boot animation color
D2 Red
D3 Azure
D6 Magenta
D4 Green
D5 Blue
D7 Cyan

The azure of D3 is a lighter blue than the blue of D5.

Held input colors

Ring patterns

These patterns cover the resting ring for a short time.

Ring Cause
Two colors, one on the top half and one on the bottom half You hold a button or a wheel direction
One white LED You turned the wheel one step
One white flash The IR receiver received a frame from a remote
Dim magenta at D6 for 3 s The IR LED transmits after the SW1 hold
One cyan flash You connected USB power
One orange flash You disconnected USB power
Dark blue ring for 0.5 s The SW3 hold armed the sleep check
Red ring for 1.5 s The board refused the sleep request
Green bar on dim blue The VU meter is on
Blue ring that fills The board installs the full firmware
