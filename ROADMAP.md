# Roadmap — c6remote

Hardware findings from schematic + PCB audit (2026-05-25), reconciled against the live design (2026-07-17). Ordered by severity.

## Critical

- [x] **ENC1 pad 6/8 function swap (root cause of dead up, center press, and rotation).** Confirmed 2026-07-21 by geometric match of `Library.pretty/Ano Rotary.kicad_mod` against Adafruit's own `ENCODER_ANO` Eagle footprint (all 9 pad radii match to 2 decimals, single 45 degree rotation, no mirror; sources: learn.adafruit.com/ano-rotary-encoder/pinouts, github.com/adafruit/Adafruit-ANO-Rotary-Navigation-Encoder-Breakout-PCB). Pad 6 (was wired to `ano_sw2`, U3 P14) is physically COM_A, the shared return for center push S1 and both encoder channels; pad 8 (was wired to GND as a second common) is physically S4, a switch contact. COM_A floating on a GPIO net killed center press and rotation; the pad 8 switch was hard-tied to GND. Netlist fault, identical on both units. Field-confirmed 2026-07-21: jumper pad 6 to pad 1 (GND) restored center press and rotation on unit 1. Resolved on board, verified 2026-07-26: pad 6 now on GND, pad 8 on `ano_sw2`.
  - [x] Design fix for next rev: symbol pins renamed to datasheet functions (COM_A, COM_B, S1_CENTER, S2_DOWN, S3_RIGHT, S4_UP, S5_LEFT, ENCA, ENCB) with pin numbers 6/8 swapped so existing schematic GND symbol and `ano_sw2` label land on correct pads; library and embedded schematic copies synced (clears old ERC symbol-mismatch item); board pads resynced (pad 6 GND, pad 8 `ano_sw2`); old pad 6 stub deleted, new F.Cu route from `ano_sw2` trace at (52.0, 61.2) to pad 8; zones refilled. ERC 0, DRC 0 errors 41 pre-existing warnings, 0 unconnected, parity 45 (baseline). NOTE: MCP `sync_schematic_to_board` also bogusly reassigned 5 pads and 4 segments to virtual net PWR_FLAG (U1 BAT, MK1 VDD, U2-Vs rail); hand-reverted, do not trust that tool's net resolution near PWR_FLAG symbols.
  - [x] Firmware direction remap: ENC1 mounts rotated 90 CW vs part-relative names (device-up = S5, device-down = S3, confirmed by two field observations). Yaml ports 11-15 renamed: P13 Down, P14 Right, P15 Press, P16 Left, P17 Up. P14/P16 inferred from rotation, verify after pad 8 rework.
  - [x] Prototype unit 1: full rework done and field-verified working 2026-07-27 (pad 6 trace cut, pad 8 freed from GND pour, jumpered to `ano_sw2` stub). Encoder functions confirmed on hardware.  - [x] Regenerate fab outputs after rework validation confirms mapping. Mapping confirmed on unit 1 2026-07-27; `export/` already current, pre-commit hook regenerated it with the fixed board at db23447, no drift.
  - [x] Optional: `ano_enc1`/`ano_enc2` land on ENCB/ENCA respectively (names crossed); only affects scroll count direction. Scroll direction verified correct on unit 1 2026-07-27, no yaml `pin_a`/`pin_b` swap needed.
- [x] **Fix SW1-11 TL3315 terminal mapping short.** First prototype bring-up on 2026-07-20 confirmed PCF8575 communication, but SW1-8 and SW10-11 read permanently pressed while all five ANO encoder switches read released. TL3315NF160Q terminals 1 and 2 are internally common, as are terminals 3 and 4. Current footprint assigns GND to pad 1 and `sw*` to pad 2, shorting each populated switch input directly to GND. SW9 reads released, likely from an open or incomplete joint, and does not invalidate the mapping fault. Prototype rework: cut each `sw*` F.Cu trace immediately before pad 2, then jumper trace side to unused pad 4. Verify released input is open to GND and pressed input is near 0 ohms on one switch before repeating. Next revision: assign GND to pads 1 and 2, and `sw*` to pads 3 and 4 in schematic symbol, footprint, and PCB. Resolved on board, verified 2026-07-26: all 11 switches read pads 1 and 2 on GND, pads 3 and 4 on `sw<n>`, checked individually.
  - [x] Schematic done (2026-07-21): replaced generic 2-pin `Switch:SW_Push` with 4-pin `Local:SW_TL3315NF160Q` (new `Local.kicad_sym`, registered in sym-lib-table). Pins 1,2 stacked on GND node, pins 3,4 stacked on `sw*` node. Netlist verified all 11 SW: pads 1,2 GND, pads 3,4 `sw*`. ERC 0. Board copper unchanged (still old mapping) so schematic-vs-board parity now diverges by design until PCB is reworked/regenerated.
  - [x] Board copper remapped, verified 2026-07-26 (see parent). Prototype units still need the hand-rework above, then fab regen. Do NOT sync_schematic_to_board blindly (would reroute copper).
- [x] **Add IR LED current-limit resistor (D1).** ~~No series R between +3.3V and D1 — only base R1 (470Ω) on Q1. Will destroy IR LED or transistor.~~ Added R3 22Ω 0603 in series between +3.3V and D1 anode; placed on PCB at (55, 33.258) F.Cu with trace to D1 anode and via to B.Cu +3.3V pour. DRC clean for this change.

## High

- [x] **Add I²C pull-ups.** ~~SDA/SCL had no external pull-ups; ESP32-C6 internal pulls too weak for PCF8575 + 16 keys + rotary.~~ R4 4.7k SDA→+3.3V, R5 4.7k SCL→+3.3V added and placed on F.Cu near U1/U3.
- [x] **Decide D2 SK6812 power rail.** ~~`VCC = U1/14 (VBUS)` would leave LED dead on battery-only.~~ Rewired D2/4 and C2/1 to +3.3V — LED works on battery. (VCC label on U1.14/VBUS is a dangling single-node net; rename to `VBUS` for clarity someday.)
- [x] **D2 runs below datasheet VDD (3.3V rail, 3.7V minimum part).** Found 2026-07-25: `SK68XX MINI` rev 08 specs VDD 3.7-5.5 and `WS2812B-2020` V1.3 specs 3.7-5.3, so old and new status LED both sit under spec on the +3.3V rail the VBUS rewire above moved them to. Data input unaffected (WS2812B-2020 VIH min is an absolute 2.7V, not 0.7*VDD, so 3.3V GPIO clears it). Expected symptom: dim or color-shifted blue (blue Vf 2.7-3.0V), not failure. Quiescent draw <0.6mA continuous with LED dark, the dominant idle load on a battery remote. Closed 2026-07-26, accepted, no fix: prototype ran the SK6812MINI (same 3.7V min, same 12mA/channel class) at 3.3V fine, and the LEDs are state display, so color choice on real hardware absorbs the shift. Capping brightness does not help, WS2812 parts dim by PWM duty at constant current, so Vf headroom is unchanged; mitigation is color choice. Still unverified: the 4-long cascade, three DOUT to DIN handoffs at ~3.3V swing against 2.7V VIH, confirm all four light at next bring-up. Rail options priced out 2026-07-26, all rejected: `VCC` (U1/14 `VBUS_14`, 4.75-5.25V) is zero on battery; `BAT` (U1/32, unregulated 3.0-4.2V) is in spec only for the top third of a LiPo curve, worse than the LDO at 3.0-3.3V, and drifts LED color across a charge cycle, worse for an indicator than a constant small deficit; a 0.3V Schottky diode-OR of VBUS and BAT leaves BAT at 3.4V, still under, the drop eats the headroom where it is needed; a 5V boost is in spec in every state but costs board area, runs continuously, and has no pin left to enable it (all 16 expander IO and all 11 XIAO GPIO pads used). The regulated 3V3 LDO output stays. If revisited, swap the part, not the rail.
- [x] **Look for a 3.3V-rated addressable RGB LED (optional, supersedes the rail hunt).** Raised 2026-07-26: a part rated at or below 3.3V is cheaper than any rail change (all rail options rejected above), but it has to hold roughly the 2020 PLCC4 size (4.0mm2, chosen over the old 3535 at 12.25mm2). Searched 2026-07-26. Exactly one 2020 addressable RGB is datasheet-rated to 3.3V: Dialight `587-1024-147F` (3.3-5.5V, 2.0x2.0x0.9mm, single wire, 5mA, POR 2.6V, corner-for-corner identical to `WS2812B-2020` so copper would not move, only pad numbering differs: VDD pin 2, GND pin 4). Rejected on sourcing, not merit: LCSC `C22471867` and JLCPCB stock 0, DigiKey 12,743 at $0.85/1 ($0.339/1000) with 15-week lead, 5-7x the Chinese parts. Also checked and rejected: `WS2812C-2020-V1` (`C2976072`, 481k stock, 3.7-5.3V, VIH 0.7*VDD, no gain), `SK6812-2020`/`EC20-6812` (characterized only at 4.5-5.5V, zero LCSC results), `TX1812Z5` (stock 0 and 30, 3.3V only empirically), `IN-PI554FCH` (`C22219545` stock 0, 5050 at 25mm2, out on size, the usual 3.3V recommendation). No 3535 pursued once a 2020 candidate existed. Physical reason nothing is rated below 3.5V: blue InGaN Vf is 2.8-3.0V at 5-12mA and the constant-current sink needs headroom above that, so at 3.3V the sink is in dropout, only a boost changes that. Resolved by the best in-stock compromise, see the swap item below.
- [x] **Swap D2-D5 to `XL-2020RGBC-WS2812B` and cut a matching land pattern.** Done 2026-07-26, closes the search item above. XINGLIGHT, LCSC `C5349955`, 183,977 stock, $0.101/1 to $0.060/500, cheaper than the `WS2812B-2020` it replaces, whose LCSC line `C965555` is flagged discontinued at JLCPCB anyway (stock 1, successor `C52917434`). Gains: VDD 3.5-5.5V (was 3.7-5.3), VIH 0.5*VDD (was absolute 2.7V), drive 5mA/ch (was 12mA, the one that matters: blue Vf falls with current, buying headroom on the 3.3V rail). Cost: roughly 3x dimmer (B 50-150 vs 100-200 mcd), acceptable for state display, already planned at 50% cap. Protocol unchanged (single wire, 800kHz, GRB high bit first, reset >100us), ESPHome keeps `chipset: WS2812`, `rgb_order: GRB`, `num_leds: 4`. Pin numbering identical (1 DO, 2 GND, 3 DI, 4 VDD) per the DigiKey-hosted datasheet revision and the board's own pad-to-net mapping, so no net remap; beware the xonstorage revision, whose pin table contradicts its own diagram (see the footprint bullet in `AGENTS.md`). Land pattern changed: this part is a 2.0x2.0mm body wanting 0.85x0.75mm pads at ±0.675 X, ±0.625 Y, where the stock 2.2x2.0 footprint had 0.7x0.7 at ±0.915, ±0.55 (new terminals overlapped only ~59% of old pads, biased outboard). New footprint `kicad lib/Library.pretty/LED_XL-2020RGBC-WS2812B_PLCC4_2.0x2.0mm.kicad_mod`: datasheet pads, silk ±1.3/±1.2, courtyard ±1.35/±1.25 (both tighter in X), 2.0x2.0 F.Fab body. Positions, rotations, uuids, nets all preserved, every track stub still lands inside its new pad, no rerouting needed. One catch: smaller pads starved D5 pad 2's thermal relief to 1 spoke against a min of 2 (locally overridden to error), so all four VSS pads now carry `zone_connect 2`, solid pour connection, lower return inductance. Validated: ERC 0, DRC 48 warnings 0 errors 0 unconnected, parity 13 `TP_*` only, all baseline. BOM regroups to one row, Qty 4. Placement: all four on a square around the ANO wheel, radius 19.046273mm from centre (53.337455, 67.714045) at 45/135/225/315 degrees (D2 bottom-left, D3 top-left, D4 top-right, D5 bottom-right), 1.85mm outside the 17.2mm wheel outline so the chain reads as a ring; rotations D2 -45, D3 -135, D4 135, D5 -135; chain copper rerouted by hand after the move. Gotcha for pad maths from the board file: KiCad applies footprint rotation as `-theta` in y-down board coords, so a naive `+theta` transform gives plausible but wrong pad positions on any footprint not at 0 or 180.
- [x] **Add TSOP IR receiver supply filter.** ~~U2 TSOP45xx missing datasheet filter.~~ R6 100Ω 0603 inline +3.3V→U2/3, C3 100nF 0603 U2/3↔U2/2. Both placed on PCB (R6 @38.5,33 / C3 @40.5,35.5) and routed — 0 unconnected items.

## Medium

- [x] **Decide PCF8575 INT (U3/1) handling.** Wired to net `exp_int` → XIAO pin24, with R9 100k pull-up to +3.3V. Interrupt-driven path in place.
- [x] **Add 100nF decoupling at U3 PCF8575 VDD.** Evaluated 2026-07-17, decided NOT to add. C1 (1µF 0805) already sits ~2mm from U3/24 on F.Cu with a short loop. PCF8575 is a slow I2C GPIO expander (400kHz bus, static outputs) with negligible HF supply demand, so C1 alone is sufficient. A 100nF would only help at HF, and the F.Cu corner around pin24 is fully routed (SDA/SCL fanout to R4/R5), so the only free spot was B.Cu-through-vias, whose via inductance negates the low-ESL HF benefit. Not worth the redundancy. Prototype C4 addition was reverted.
- [x] **Shrink and simplify the D2 status LED block.** Was 3 parts (SK6812MINI 3535 12.25mm² + R2 330Ω series on `led_1` + C2 1uF 0805), now 1: a 2020 PLCC4 LED alone, 4.0mm² for the size win (final part `XL-2020RGBC-WS2812B`, see the swap item under High; a `WS2812B-2020` stopgap lived one day in between). R2 deleted: 330Ω is a current-limit value, not the 22-100Ω data-line damping value, and ~5mm of trace needs none, GPIO17 drives DIN directly. C2 deleted: the layout feeds the LEDs off the planes (see the LED chain decoupling item below). Sourcing fixed too: old line was `Adafruit / MPN 4960`, a 10-pack SKU not a reel. ESPHome `chipset:` SK6812 to WS2812. Schematic verified 2026-07-25: ERC 0, netlist delta exactly R2 gone and `Net-(D2-DIN)` merged into `led_1` (43 to 41 nets). Board done 2026-07-26: footprint swapped, R2 pads and stubs gone, `led_1` rerouted to DIN, zones refilled, DRC 0 errors.
- [x] **Extend status light to a 4-LED chain.** Added D3-D5 (same part, footprint, sourcing as D2) cascaded off D2: `GPIO17` → `led_1` → D2 DIN, then DOUT → `led_2`/`led_3`/`led_4` → next DIN, D5 DOUT no-connect. One GPIO, three new nets, no existing net touched (schematic verified 2026-07-25: ERC 0, netlist 41 to 44 nets). All four VDD on `+3.3V`, VSS on GND, no decoupling caps (see below). ESPHome `num_leds: 1` to `4`. Budget: 60mA peak full white at the final part's 5mA/ch (144mA when planned at 12mA/ch) on the 700mA XIAO 3V3 rail, 2.4mA quiescent dark, now the dominant idle load. Board done 2026-07-26: D3-D5 placed, three hops routed, zones refilled, DRC 0 errors 0 unconnected.
- [x] **Decide LED chain decoupling.** Decided 2026-07-26: NO caps at all, C2 (the last one) deleted, because the layout already feeds each LED off the planes. Every VDD pad drops into the board-wide B.Cu `+3.3V` plane through a via 0.00mm (D3, on-pad), 1.17mm (D5), 1.92mm (D4), 4.41mm (D2) away, and every VSS pad sits solid in the F.Cu GND pour, so each supply loop is a few nH and current comes off the plane, not down a trace to a cap. At WS2812 edge rates with ~20mA per-channel steps that is single-digit mV droop, a local 100nF buys nothing measurable. Two paper arguments were wrong: distance to C2 (22-39mm) never mattered because C2 was not in the current path, and a cap cannot help the under-spec VDD item under High, 3.3V against a 3.7V minimum is a DC shortfall and decoupling only supplies transients. Plane-pair capacitance is not the mechanism either, ~2500mm2 over 1.6mm FR4 is only ~60pF. C2 removal stranded nothing: schematic `power:+3.3V` at 123.19,151.13 still feeds D2 pin 4 through the junction at 123.19,153.67 and `power:GND` at 152.4,165.1 feeds pin 2; board kept both `+3.3V` F.Cu tracks, they meet at 42.975,85.0 and carry D2 supply to the via at 43.0,86.25. ERC 0, DRC 0 errors 0 unconnected, unchanged. If bring-up shows LED trouble at 3.3V, fix the rail (VBUS or a boost), not the decoupling.
- [ ] **Add rotary encoder pull-ups (optional).** `ano_enc1/2` go straight ENC1→XIAO (ENC1/7→U1/10, ENC1/3→U1/9) with no external pulls, relying on MCU internal. Add 10kΩ 0603 to +3.3V on each channel for robustness; optional 10nF to GND for hardware debounce.
- [x] **Fix SW1-11 button footprint / 3D model to match sourced part.** ~~Board carries E-Switch `SW_TL3315NF160Q` but assumed assembled part was a generic 6mm tactile.~~ Resolved 2026-07-17: PCBWay confirmed by email (Freya, 2026-07-16) they purchased the exact TL3315NF160Q from the BOM, and the assembly photo shows the matching SMD part. Footprint and BOM were already correct. 3D model was wrong part entirely: `SW_SPST_PTS647Sx50_black.step` (5.0mm tall plunger) replaced with generated `3dmodels/TL3315NF160Q.wrl` built from the TL3315 datasheet drawing (4.5x4.5x0.55 ultra low profile, dark gray housing, 4.0 dia gold snap dome, silver gull wings at 4.75 span) in the lib footprint and all 11 board instances; old step deleted; readme board renders regenerated. Manufacturer STEP was rejected: it is a flat uncolored slab with no dome. Note: WRL renders in KiCad but is excluded from board STEP exports for MCAD - at 0.55mm tall the switches are negligible for enclosure work. The two `SW_PUSH_6mm` THT swap stashes ("SW1-11 footprint swap TL3315->SW_PUSH_6mm" and "WIP button THT swap") are obsolete and can be dropped; a THT swap would also have required ~20 track reroutes around new drill holes and a 90-degree contact-orientation fix, all moot now.
