What homeThing C6 does.

For your TV, Sonos, Zigbee lights and Home Assistant.
No touchscreen, no apps, no daily charging.

  • Runs your TV

    Learns the IR codes from the remote you already have. Then the wheel and pad drive the TV.

  • Switches the lights

    A button talks straight to Zigbee lights and groups.

  • No hub needed

    Presses go straight to your Zigbee lights and IR devices. They work without Wi-Fi, Home Assistant or Zigbee2MQTT.

  • Sleeps on its own

    It sleeps between presses, aimed at about a month per charge.

  • No flashing needed

    Set every button from the remote's own web page, in any browser. No YAML to edit and no firmware to rebuild.

    homething-c6-13f498.local

  • Any button, any device

    Each button can drive something different. Here one lights the fireplace over Zigbee.

  • Bluetooth keys too

    Pair it like a Bluetooth keyboard or gamepad, and send media keys to a computer, phone or tablet.

  • Open source, all of it

    The board, the case and the ESPHome firmware are on GitHub. Build one, change it or fix it.

Specs

One remote, no screen.

  • Controls Scroll wheel with 5-way pad, 11 assignable buttons
  • Battery 1000 mAh LiPo, aimed at about a month per charge
  • Charging USB-C
  • Processor Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32-C6
  • Infrared Transmits, receives and learns codes
  • Voice Microphone for the Home Assistant voice assistant
  • Status 4 RGB LEDs
  • Case 3D printed, SLS or FDM
  • Wireless Wi-Fi BLE Zigbee

Your smart home, on real buttons.

Control Anything

Pick it up, press a button, and the lights change. No phone to unlock, no app to open.

TV: Learns the IR codes from the remote you already have, then sends them.

Music: Controls Sonos over Wi-Fi, or pairs with a phone, tablet or computer over BLE for media keys.

Lights: Talks straight to Zigbee lights and groups.

Home Assistant: Puts any action or script on a button.

Voice: Talks to your Home Assistant voice assistant through the built-in mic.

Built Your Way

A custom board in an open case.

Custom PCB built around the Seeed Studio XIAO ESP32-C6.

Parametric 3D printed case in SLS and FDM versions.

Make It Yours

Every button is yours to set.

Open the remote’s own web page from any browser on your network. Pick a button, then pick what it does: an IR code, a Zigbee light or group, a BLE media key, a Home Assistant action, or voice.

You can also assign buttons on the remote itself. No app, no cloud.
