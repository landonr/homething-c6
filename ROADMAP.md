# Roadmap — c6remote

Hardware findings from schematic + PCB audit (2026-05-25), reconciled against the live design (2026-07-17). Ordered by severity.

## Critical

- [x] **ENC1 pad 6/8 function swap (root cause of dead up, center press, and rotation).** Confirmed 2026-07-21 by geometric match of `Library.pretty/Ano Rotary.kicad_mod` against Adafruit's own `ENCODER_ANO` Eagle footprint (all 9 pad radii match to 2 decimals, single 45 degree rotation, no mirror; sources: learn.adafruit.com/ano-rotary-encoder/pinouts, github.com/adafruit/Adafruit-ANO-Rotary-Navigation-Encoder-Breakout-PCB). Pad 6 (was wired to `ano_sw2`, U3 P14) is physically COM_A, the shared return for center push S1 and both encoder channels; pad 8 (was wired to GND as a second common) is physically S4, a switch contact. COM_A floating on a GPIO net killed center press and rotation; the pad 8 switch was hard-tied to GND. Netlist fault, identical on both units. Field-confirmed 2026-07-21: jumper pad 6 to pad 1 (GND) restored center press and rotation on unit 1. Resolved on the board, verified 2026-07-26: ENC1 pad 6 now sits on GND and pad 8 on `ano_sw2`, which is the swap this item asked for.

## High

- [x] **Add I²C pull-ups.** ~~SDA/SCL had no external pull-ups; ESP32-C6 internal pulls too weak for PCF8575 + 16 keys + rotary.~~ R4 4.7k SDA→+3.3V, R5 4.7k SCL→+3.3V added and placed on F.Cu near U1/U3.
- [x] **Decide D2 SK6812 power rail.** ~~`VCC = U1/14 (VBUS)` would leave LED dead on battery-only.~~ Rewired D2/4 and C2/1 to +3.3V — LED works on battery. (VCC label on U1.14/VBUS is a dangling single-node net; rename to `VBUS` for clarity someday.)
- [x] **D2 runs below datasheet VDD (3.3V rail, 3.7V minimum part).** Found 2026-07-25 while picking a smaller LED. Normand `SK68XX MINI` rev 08 specs VDD +3.7 to +5.5, Worldsemi `WS2812B-2020` V1.3 specs VDD +3.7 to +5.3, so both the old and the new status LED sit under spec on the +3.3V rail that the VBUS rewire above moved them to. Data input is unaffected (WS2812B-2020 VIH min is an absolute 2.7V, not 0.7*VDD, so a 3.3V GPIO clears it). Expected symptom is dim or color-shifted blue (blue Vf 2.7-3.0V), not failure. Confirm on prototype before considering a fix; the fix costs a rail (VBUS with battery fallback, or a boost) so accept dim blue unless bring-up shows worse. Related: quiescent draw is <0.6mA continuous even with the LED dark, which is the dominant idle load on a battery remote. Closed 2026-07-26, accepted, no fix. Prototype bring-up ran the SK6812MINI (same 3.7V datasheet minimum, same 12mA/channel class) at 3.3V with no trouble, and the LEDs are state display rather than illumination, so whatever blue shift the 0.4V shortfall causes gets absorbed by picking colors that read correctly on real hardware. Note that capping brightness does not change the headroom, since WS2812 parts dim by PWM duty at constant current, so the mitigation is color choice, not brightness. The one LED behaviour never built and so still unverified is the 4-long cascade: three DOUT to DIN handoffs, each retimed off its own supply, with VIH min an absolute 2.7V against a roughly 3.3V DOUT swing. Confirm all four light during the next bring-up. Rail options priced out 2026-07-26, all rejected. Both higher rails already exist as nets: `VCC` is U1 pad 14 (`VBUS_14`, 4.75 to 5.25V but zero on battery, which is why the item above moved off it) and `BAT` is U1 pad 32 (`BAT_32`, unregulated 3.0 to 4.2V). BAT looks attractive and is not: a LiPo spends most of its usable curve between 3.6 and 4.0V, so against a 3.7V minimum it is in spec for roughly the top third of the pack and under it thereafter, and at 3.0 to 3.3V it is worse than the LDO. Being unregulated it would also drift LED brightness and colour across a charge cycle, so the blue state would visibly change as the pack drains, which is a worse failure mode for an indicator than a constant small deficit. Diode-OR of VBUS and BAT does not rescue it either: a 0.3V Schottky drop leaves VBUS at 4.7V (fine) but BAT at 3.7V becomes 3.4V, still under, so the drop eats the headroom exactly where it is needed. A 5V boost is the only option in spec in every state and costs board area plus a continuously running converter, because there is no pin left to enable it with (all 16 expander IO and all 11 XIAO GPIO pads are used). Conclusion: the regulated 3V3 LDO output is the most stable rail on the board and stays. If this is ever revisited, swap the part, not the rail.
- [x] **Look for a 3.3V-rated addressable RGB LED (optional, supersedes the rail hunt).** Raised 2026-07-26 once the plan settled on running blue at up to 50% on the 3.3V rail. Swapping to a part whose datasheet VDD minimum is at or below 3.3V is cheaper than any rail change (see the item above, all rail options rejected). Constraint that matters: D2-D5 are `WS2812B-2020`, a 2020 PLCC4 at 4.0mm2, chosen deliberately over the old 3535 at 12.25mm2, so a candidate has to hold roughly that size. The part usually named for 3.3V work, Inolux `IN-PI554FCH`, is 5050 at 25mm2, over 6x the area per LED and 4 LEDs to place, so it is very likely a non-starter on this board. Searched 2026-07-26. Result: exactly one 2020 addressable RGB is datasheet-rated to 3.3V, and it has no LCSC stock. Dialight `587-1024-147F` (587 series, 2.0x2.0x0.9mm, single wire, 5mA drive, power-on-reset 2.6V, -40 to +85C) specs operating voltage 3.3-5.5V and is corner-for-corner identical to `WS2812B-2020` (DI top-left, GND top-right, VDD bottom-left, DO bottom-right), so copper would not move, only pad numbering differs (it calls VDD pin 2 and GND pin 4). But LCSC `C22471867` stock is 0 and JLCPCB stock is 0; DigiKey has 12,743 at $0.85/1, $0.433/100, $0.339/1000 with a 15-week lead, which is 5-7x the Chinese parts. Rejected on sourcing, not on merit. Also checked and rejected: `WS2812C-2020-V1` (`C2976072`, 481k stock, 3.7-5.3V, VIH 0.7*VDD, no gain), `SK6812-2020`/`EC20-6812` (abs max 3.5-5.5V but characterized only at 4.5-5.5V, VIH 0.7*VDD, zero LCSC results), `TX1812Z5` (`C784564` stock 0, `C2943067` stock 30, 0.7*VDD on paper and 3.3V only empirically), and `IN-PI554FCH` (`C22219545` stock 0, $0.62, 5050 at 25mm2, already out on size). No 3535 was worth pursuing once a 2020 candidate existed. Physical reason nothing is rated below 3.5V: blue InGaN Vf is 2.8-3.0V at 5-12mA and the constant-current sink needs headroom above that, so at 3.3V the sink is in dropout. Only a boost changes that, and there is no pin left to enable one. Resolved by taking the best in-stock compromise instead, see the swap item below. Incidental finding: `C965555`, the `WS2812B-2020` LCSC part this BOM used, is flagged 停产 (discontinued) in JLCPCB's catalog with stock 1, successor `WS2812B-2020-V6` = `C52917434` with 47,466 stock. Moot now that D2-D5 moved off it, but it means the old BOM line was on an EOL part.
- [x] **Swap D2-D5 to `XL-2020RGBC-WS2812B` and cut a matching land pattern.** Done 2026-07-26, closing out the search item above. XINGLIGHT `XL-2020RGBC-WS2812B`, LCSC `C5349955`, 183,977 in stock, $0.101/1 down to $0.060/500, so cheaper than the `WS2812B-2020` it replaces. Gains against the old part: rated VDD 3.5-5.5V instead of 3.7-5.3V, VIH specified as 0.5*VDD instead of an absolute 2.7V, drive current 5mA/ch instead of 12mA. The lower drive current is the one that matters, because blue Vf falls with current and that is what buys headroom on the 3.3V rail. Cost is brightness: R 50-150, G 200-400, B 50-150 mcd at 5mA against R 150-250, G 500-700, B 100-200 at 12mA, roughly 3x dimmer, which is acceptable for state display and was already the plan at 50% cap. Protocol is unchanged (single wire, 800kHz, GRB high bit first, reset >100us) so ESPHome keeps `chipset: WS2812`, `rgb_order: GRB`, `num_leds: 4` with no edit. Pin numbering is identical to `WS2812B-2020` (1 DO, 2 GND, 3 DI, 4 VDD), verified against the DigiKey-hosted datasheet revision and against the board's own pad-to-net mapping, so no net remapping and no schematic topology change. Beware the xonstorage datasheet revision, whose pin function table contradicts its own pin diagram; see the footprint bullet in `AGENTS.md`. The land pattern did have to change: `WS2812B-2020` is a 2.2x2.0mm body so KiCad's stock footprint puts 0.7x0.7mm pads at ±0.915 X and ±0.55 Y, while this part is 2.0x2.0mm and wants 0.85x0.75mm pads at ±0.675 X and ±0.625 Y. Against the old pads the new terminals only overlapped about 59% of their area, biased outboard. New footprint `kicad lib/Library.pretty/LED_XL-2020RGBC-WS2812B_PLCC4_2.0x2.0mm.kicad_mod` carries the datasheet pattern plus silk at ±1.3/±1.2, courtyard at ±1.35/±1.25 (both smaller in X than the stock part, so less silk and courtyard pressure), and a 2.0x2.0 F.Fab body. Footprint positions, rotations, uuids and nets all preserved; every signal and power track stub still lands inside its new pad, so nothing needed rerouting. One real catch: shrinking the pads starved D5 pad 2's thermal relief to a single spoke against a min of 2, which is a locally-overridden error rather than a warning, so all four VSS pads now carry `zone_connect 2` for a solid pour connection. That also makes true the claim `AGENTS.md` already made about the VSS pads sitting directly in the F.Cu GND pour, and lowers the return-path inductance. Validated: ERC 0, DRC 48 violations 0 errors 0 unconnected, parity 13 (all `TP_*` `extra_footprint`), all exactly the pre-existing baseline. BOM regroups to one row, Reference `D2, D3, D4, D5`, Qty 4.

## Medium

- [x] **Decide PCF8575 INT (U3/1) handling.** Wired to net `exp_int` → XIAO pin24, with R9 100k pull-up to +3.3V. Interrupt-driven path in place.
- [x] **Add 100nF decoupling at U3 PCF8575 VDD.** Evaluated 2026-07-17, decided NOT to add. C1 (1µF 0805) already sits ~2mm from U3/24 on F.Cu with a short loop. PCF8575 is a slow I2C GPIO expander (400kHz bus, static outputs) with negligible HF supply demand, so C1 alone is sufficient. A 100nF would only help at HF, and the F.Cu corner around pin24 is fully routed (SDA/SCL fanout to R4/R5), so the only free spot was B.Cu-through-vias, whose via inductance negates the low-ESL HF benefit. Not worth the redundancy. Prototype C4 addition was reverted.
- [x] **Shrink and simplify the D2 status LED block.** Was 3 parts: SK6812MINI 3535 (12.25mm²) + R2 330Ω series on `led_1` + C2 1uF 0805. Now 2 parts. D2 → `WS2812B-2020` (Worldsemi, LCSC C965555, ~$0.05/reel part, 2.0x2.0 PLCC4, 4.0mm², same 1-wire 800kbit protocol, same 12mA/channel die class). Pin numbering is identical between the two KiCad symbols (1 DOUT, 2 VSS, 3 DIN, 4 VDD) and matches the WS2812B-2020 datasheet pin table, so no net remapping. Also replaces the old sourcing line, which was `Manufacturer Adafruit / MPN 4960`, an Adafruit 10-pack SKU rather than a reel an assembler can buy. R2 deleted: 330Ω is an LED current-limit value, not the 22-100Ω damping value a data line wants, and at ~5mm of trace on one rail no series R is needed; GPIO17 now drives DIN directly. C2 1uF 0805 went to 100nF 0603 (`CC0603KRX7R9BB104`) and then deleted outright on 2026-07-26 (see the LED decoupling item below), so the block is now 1 part. Schematic verified 2026-07-25: ERC 0, netlist delta is exactly R2 gone and `Net-(D2-DIN)` merged into `led_1` (43 nets to 41), component delta exactly D2 value/footprint, C2 value/footprint, R2 removed. ESPHome `chipset:` moved `SK6812` to `WS2812`. Board done 2026-07-26: D2 footprint swapped, R2 pads and stubs gone, `led_1` rerouted to DIN, zones refilled, DRC 0 errors. Part superseded later the same day, see the `XL-2020RGBC-WS2812B` swap item under High.
- [x] **Extend status light to a 4-LED chain.** Added D3, D4, D5 (`WS2812B-2020`, same footprint and sourcing as D2) cascaded off D2: `GPIO17` → `led_1` → D2 DIN, D2 DOUT → `led_2` → D3 DIN, D3 DOUT → `led_3` → D4 DIN, D4 DOUT → `led_4` → D5 DIN, D5 DOUT no-connect. Still one GPIO, no new nets outside the three chain nets. All four VDD on `+3.3V`, all four VSS on GND, no decoupling caps (see the item below). Schematic verified 2026-07-25: ERC 0, netlist 41 nets to 44, added nets exactly `led_2`/`led_3`/`led_4`, no existing net touched. ESPHome `num_leds: 1` to `4`. Budget: 144mA peak at full white on a 700mA XIAO 3V3 rail, and 2.4mA quiescent with all four dark, which is now the dominant idle load. Board done 2026-07-26: D3-D5 placed, three chain hops routed, zones refilled, DRC 0 errors 0 unconnected. Part superseded later the same day, see the `XL-2020RGBC-WS2812B` swap item under High.
- [x] **Decide LED chain decoupling.** Evaluated 2026-07-26, decided NO caps at all, and deleted C2 (the last one). The earlier plan carried one 100nF for the group, with per-LED caps as the fallback if the chain misbehaved. Measuring the finished layout killed both. Every LED VDD pad drops into the board-wide B.Cu `+3.3V` plane (extent 35.3,28.7 to 71.3,157.7) through a via 0.00mm (D3, via on the pad), 1.17mm (D5), 1.92mm (D4) and 4.41mm (D2) away, and every VSS pad sits directly in the F.Cu GND pour, so each supply loop is a few nH. Current never traverses a long trace to a cap, it comes off the plane. At WS2812 edge rates with ~20mA per-channel steps that is single-digit millivolts of droop, so a local 100nF buys nothing measurable. Two arguments that looked good on paper were wrong: distance to C2 (22 to 39mm) is irrelevant because C2 was not in the current path, and a cap cannot help the under-spec VDD item under High, since 3.3V against a 3.7V minimum is a DC shortfall and decoupling only supplies transients. Plane-pair capacitance is not the reason either, at 1.6mm FR4 over ~2500mm2 of overlap it is only ~60pF. Deleting C2: schematic C2 symbol plus its two GND wires removed (the `power:+3.3V` at 123.19,151.13 feeds D2 pin4 through the junction at 123.19,153.67 directly, and `power:GND` at 152.4,165.1 feeds D2 pin2, so nothing was stranded), board footprint removed while keeping both `+3.3V` F.Cu tracks because they meet each other at 42.975,85.0 and carry D2's supply to the via at 43.0,86.25. ERC 0, DRC 0 errors 0 unconnected, unchanged from before the deletion. If bring-up does show LED trouble at 3.3V, fix the rail (VBUS or a boost), not the decoupling.
- [ ] **Add rotary encoder pull-ups (optional).** `ano_enc1/2` go straight ENC1→XIAO (ENC1/7→U1/10, ENC1/3→U1/9) with no external pulls, relying on MCU internal. Add 10kΩ 0603 to +3.3V on each channel for robustness; optional 10nF to GND for hardware debounce.
- [x] **Fix SW1-11 button footprint / 3D model to match sourced part.** ~~Board carries E-Switch `SW_TL3315NF160Q` but assumed assembled part was a generic 6mm tactile.~~ Resolved 2026-07-17: PCBWay confirmed by email (Freya, 2026-07-16) they purchased the exact TL3315NF160Q from the BOM, and the assembly photo shows the matching SMD part. Footprint and BOM were already correct. 3D model was wrong part entirely: `SW_SPST_PTS647Sx50_black.step` (5.0mm tall plunger) replaced with generated `3dmodels/TL3315NF160Q.wrl` built from the TL3315 datasheet drawing (4.5x4.5x0.55 ultra low profile, dark gray housing, 4.0 dia gold snap dome, silver gull wings at 4.75 span) in the lib footprint and all 11 board instances; old step deleted; readme board renders regenerated. Manufacturer STEP was rejected: it is a flat uncolored slab with no dome. Note: WRL renders in KiCad but is excluded from board STEP exports for MCAD - at 0.55mm tall the switches are negligible for enclosure work. The two `SW_PUSH_6mm` THT swap stashes ("SW1-11 footprint swap TL3315->SW_PUSH_6mm" and "WIP button THT swap") are obsolete and can be dropped; a THT swap would also have required ~20 track reroutes around new drill holes and a 90-degree contact-orientation fix, all moot now.
